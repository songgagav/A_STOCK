# H5i technical-factor IC diagnostic contract

**Status:** Approved by user 2026-10-18. This document does not authorize implementation or production promotion.

## 1. Purpose and boundary

Build a reproducible, point-in-time (PIT) *diagnostic* IC curve for the two technical scores used by the legacy selector: low volatility (`vol`) and reverse 20-bar momentum (`mom_rev`). The result answers whether those score directions have recent cross-sectional association with subsequent returns. It is not a repair of the five-factor production IC feed, a selector-parity backtest, a weight update, or an Observation-day qualification.

The only approved phase is an offline CLI/research module in an isolated branch. It reads H5i and the explicitly selected local calendar read-only, then writes a new immutable diagnostic bundle. It must not change `src/ic_curve_refresh.py`, `src/ic_backtest.py`, `src/run_daily.py`, `src/weight_optimizer.py`, `src/premarket_healthcheck.py`, `src/selector.py`, `data/ic/`, `data/weights.json`, the frozen selector-weight snapshot, a running service, or an Observation Epoch. No scheduler or dashboard hookup is part of this phase.

The deployed paper/shadow contract remains `RANK_BY_FUSION=0`, `DRL_PLAN_MODE=shadow`, `FUSION_WEIGHT_MODE=shadow`, `TRADE_BROKER=paper`, and Alpha `not_promotable`. PR #23's IC-history classification and the separate industry money-flow freshness issue do not become solved by this work. Qualified Observation days remain zero until the full health/deployment/epoch gates pass.

## 2. Why an isolated producer

`ic_curve_refresh` still opens the retired `DUCKDB_PATH` and invokes `ic_backtest.build_factor`, which calls the nonexistent `factor_library.FACTORS` interface. Its current writer directly overwrites `data/ic/ic_curve_<factor>_k20.csv`. It only names `vol` and raw `mom_20`, while `premarket_healthcheck` and `weight_optimizer` expect five files. The optimizer reads `ic_h5` by default, whereas curve health tests `ic_h20` freshness. A database-path substitution would therefore leave both a runtime failure and an unreviewed factor/consumer contract.

Approaches considered:

1. Patch the old DuckDB path and registry in place. Rejected for this phase: it would immediately feed changed curves to consumers without establishing score direction, adjustment, PIT universe, or write safety.
2. Produce separate H5i diagnostics for `vol` and `mom_rev`. Selected: it tests a precise source and time contract while leaving all authoritative consumers unchanged.
3. Rebuild all five factors now. Deferred: `pb_rev` and `roe` require their own historical as-of valuation/publication contract; individual-stock `mf_net` currently lacks a valid historical source. The industry-level `money_flow_estimate` table cannot substitute for it.

## 3. Inputs, time, and sample universe

- The CLI requires explicit `--h5i-path`, `--calendar-path`, `--start`, `--end`, and `--output-root`. No implicit `today`, default production output, full-history fallback, or silent source substitution is allowed. V1 accepts only dates from 2026-05-01 onward, a recent-window diagnostic rather than a historical-all-A claim. The operator may choose a shorter interval within that bound.
- Open `h5i_db.Database(path, read_only=True)` and query `daily_bars` only. Read `(CAST(ts AS DATE), symbol, close, change_pct)` for the requested dates plus sufficient pre-start lookback. Normalize symbol/date deterministically. A duplicate `(day,symbol)` row, incompatible schema, or calendar mismatch is a run-level error, not `keep=last`.
- The calendar is the `days` array of the explicitly supplied `data/trade_calendar.json`, with its exact byte SHA-256 in the manifest. Select weekdays within the requested interval and future-label range. Because that file's upstream union version is not fully documented, label it `local_calendar_unknown_upstream`; it is not exchange-authoritative. Require `--end` no later than the source max bar day. A listed day on or before that source max with no H5i bars is a data gap and cannot be silently removed to shorten a holding period; a later calendar day is merely not yet observed.
- At each signal close `t`, the candidate universe is symbols with a valid positive `close[t]` and at least 21 valid, ordered, symbol-specific closes available no later than `t`. Do not filter with today's active-symbol list, later valuation membership, future profitability, or future return availability. This is an **all-bar-eligible diagnostic universe**, not the live selector's historically filtered/selected universe. Record eligible counts per date.
- Compute the factor using the current `factor_library.score_factor("vol"|"mom", bars_as_of_t)` formula on the last 21 available closes. Exclude insufficient/invalid histories with a counted reason; do not treat that missingness as an earned neutral `0.5`. Name the output factors `vol_selector_score_v1` and `mom_rev_selector_score_v1` to distinguish them from the optimizer's raw `vol`/`mom_20` files. Higher diagnostic score is the hypothesized favorable direction for both. The H5i close adjustment basis is recorded as `as_stored_unverified`, so this score is formula-aligned but **not** a claim of full live-selector parity.
- For each horizon `h ∈ {1,3,5,10,20}`, the forward label at `t` is `prod(1 + change_pct[d,s]/100) - 1` for exactly the next `h` calendar-listed trading days `d=t+1..t+h`. Require a finite `change_pct > -100` row for the symbol on every one of those days; do not forward-fill, substitute zero, or use `t`'s return. Missing labels remain null with reason counts. Publish non-null IC only after all `h` later trading days are on or before the source max bar day and present in the source; dates without full future maturity stay null and are not called settled.

## 4. IC and output contract

For each `(factor, t, h)`, intersect the as-of factor universe with symbols having valid matured forward labels. Use Spearman rank correlation with average ranks for ties. Require at least five paired symbols and non-constant ranks on both sides; otherwise IC is null, never zero. Each factor CSV is **long-form**, one row per `(day,horizon)`, with `day,horizon,ic,status,n_factor,n_forward_valid,n_pairs,n_missing_factor,n_missing_forward`. Here `n_factor` is the count of valid as-of factor scores; `n_forward_valid` is the count of valid forward labels among all positive-close symbols on `t`; `n_pairs` is their intersection. The two missing counts retain their explicit causes. `status` is one of `settled`, `not_mature`, `insufficient_pairs`, or `constant_rank`; a null IC is an empty field, not `0` or a textual `NaN`. Keep non-finite factor/return values out of both ranks and sample counts. The latest source bar day, calendar due day for each horizon, and last non-null settled IC day must be explicit in the manifest; an apparent CSV last row is not proof of settlement.

Reject an `--output-root` whose resolved path is inside any protected live artifact directory, especially `data/ic`, `data/evolution`, or `data/daily`. Write only beneath `<output-root>/<run_identity_sha256>/`, where the run identity hashes canonical parameters, code SHA, factor-definition version, calendar byte SHA, H5i schema fingerprint, sorted queried-row digest, and source max bar day. The directory contains `vol_selector_score_v1.csv`, `mom_rev_selector_score_v1.csv`, and a `manifest.json` with input identities, score/return formulas, horizon definitions, date range, row counts, missingness, file SHA-256s, and `diagnostic_only=true`, `authoritative=false`, `observation_eligible=false`. CSV dates are `YYYYMMDD`, rows are ordered by `(day,horizon)`, and that pair is unique. Create files in a temporary sibling directory, write the manifest last, then atomically finalize; an existing final directory is verified byte-for-byte or rejected, never overwritten.

The CLI emits machine-readable success/failure and the finalized manifest path. Missing source/calendar files, schema violations, duplicate keys, non-monotonic calendar, input mutation during the run, output collision, or an empty eligible window fail without a finalized bundle. Per-symbol absent bars/invalid percentages and per-day insufficient IC sample are recorded as explicit diagnostic exclusions; they must not be relabeled as a clean pass. A finalized bundle never enters `data/ic`, is never loaded by `weight_optimizer`, and cannot satisfy `check_ic_curve()`.

## 5. Validation and release gates

Implement with tests first. Synthetic fixtures must prove: no lookahead in 21-close factor windows; lower volatility and lower 20-bar momentum give higher scores; `t+1..t+h` labels and H5/H20 maturity are exact; ties use average rank; fewer than five pairs and constant ranks yield null; missing/invalid bars and source-day gaps are counted without filling; present-day universe membership cannot alter a historical day; duplicate rows/schema/calendar errors fail closed; repeat runs are idempotent without overwrite; and a collision or interrupted write leaves no finalized bundle.

Run focused `.venv310` tests, then the full applicable suite. Report PASS/FAIL/SKIP/NOT_RUN separately; skipped H5i/data cases are not passes. Test against a bounded real H5i window read-only and compare summary shape/direction with the legacy files **as a difference report, not a parity or promotion gate**. Before and after the real-data diagnostic, verify byte hashes for `data/ic`, `data/weights.json`, the frozen selector snapshot, and the existing Epoch; check the branch diff excludes all runtime/production paths named in section 1.

This phase is complete only when its own diagnostic bundle is reproducible and immutable, its tests and protected-path checks pass, and the result is explicitly labeled diagnostic. A separate, later decision is required to define PIT source contracts for `pb_rev`/`roe`/individual-stock `mf_net`, reconcile optimizer source-sign and H5-versus-H20 consumer semantics, replace the authoritative producer, alter health policy, deploy code, or freeze a new Observation Epoch. No result from this phase advances the five-qualified-day count.
